# OPM intent

- **Discovery.** Chapters arrive automatically, never from a pasted link. English manga comes first; the webcomic is secondary. Read both communities hourly and announce a new chapter in the round that stores it; no raw-release clock or translator-specific feed.
- **Chapters.** Each row opens its chapter at the saved page; no separate Continue Reading row.
- **Reader.** One continuous page strip grows into the next chapter as it is reached; a tap shows the system bars.
