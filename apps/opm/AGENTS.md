# OPM intent

- **Discovery.** Find chapters automatically; a pasted link is never a source. Prioritise the English manga over the webcomic. Find chapters the day they are posted. Follow communities, not a single translator.
- **Chapters.** Open each chapter at its saved page. Add no Continue Reading row. Keep a reader's place when chapters are found.
- **Reader.** Folded or upright, pages run in one continuous strip; unfolded like a book, a chapter reads as right-to-left two-page spreads turned one at a time, keep each spread within one chapter. The saved place is a page, and folding or unfolding keeps it. Add the next chapter when the reader reaches it. A tap shows the system bars.
- **Notifications.** Announce each new chapter once per device. Send no backlog when a phone registers.
