# Scope intent

- One page lists the chosen app's operations as one list, with no app groups or headings. Each 32px row leads with the operation name; failures, runs and typical duration share fixed right columns, so every row lines up, and the failures column is left out while no row in the list has failures. Failing operations lead, then the most recently seen; the same operation name from two sides is two rows, and only then does a muted 12px side glyph follow the name. An operation is a root span's name; HTTP operations use method and route or path, with unmatched requests grouped rather than one row per scanner path. Scope keeps each service's newest ordered release, but never deletes an unversioned or unordered service for it.
- The bar's section control chooses the app: one tab per app showing only its 16px mark, named for assistive technology. One app is always chosen; the URL holds it, and an open that names no app, or one this data no longer has, redirects to the app whose newest failed trace is newest, else the first tab, replacing the history entry. Choosing another tab shows only its operations and clears an operation, trace or span selection that isn't in it; choosing the chosen tab does nothing. The bar also carries the time frame as stock tabs — 10m, 1h, 24h, 3d, default 1h — and the URL holds it. A section control too wide for the bar scrolls horizontally, keeping the chosen tab in view; the time frame stays fixed before the account.
- Every pane starts with one 32px `ItemListHeading` band with its 16px icon, so its text lines up with the row names: traces with the selected operation's app mark and name, the trace pane with a muted trace glyph and the selected trace's short id and age. With nothing selected the band keeps its icon and stays otherwise empty. The chosen operation lists its slowest traces with its newest failures among them. Each trace row is 32px: age, a short trace ID with its failure dot immediately after, duration. Hover names the failing span; selecting the trace opens that span's diagnostic, not a repeated message on the row. Only top-level traces are rows. New arrivals appear directly, without animating row height or rescanning every trace merely to animate it. One stale mark in the bar carries the oldest age among the operations and trace readers, and its retry refreshes both; no pane repeats it. The frame filters by a trace's root start: operations with no trace inside it leave the list, while every app with retained traces keeps its tab, and their failures, runs, typical duration and rows count only in-frame traces. A new frame keeps a trace it still holds and clears one outside; an operation with no trace left falls back. No traces in the frame reads `No traces in this frame`.
- The trace pane is a flame: under its heading band a 24px time axis marks 0, the quarters and the total in tabular figures, its first label left-aligned at 0, its last right-aligned at the end and the rest centred on their ticks, and the axis stays pinned at the pane's top while the spans scroll. Lanes fill the pane's width, never scrolling sideways: the root takes the first, each job (a root's direct child) the next with its after-label's space kept free, and each deeper span the first lane whose time is free, so no two spans in a lane overlap; bars are 22px tall with 2px between lanes, and a 1px background seam at a bar's right edge keeps touching bars distinct, never on a 2px tick. Each span is a bar positioned and sized on the trace's time scale, filled with the hue of the app that ran it and destructive red when it failed. The root and each job show their name and duration inside the bar while the full label fits, otherwise after the bar in the lane's free time while it fits before the next bar and the pane's edge, otherwise not at all; a deeper span's name is in its detail, never a clipped label, and every span's parent job, name and duration stay its title and accessible name. A span shorter than 2px is a 2px tick, and every bar's tap target is at least 12px wide. Clicking a bar, or pressing Enter, picks it: a 1px foreground outline inside the bar, the span in the URL, and below one hairline its app's mark, its name (destructive when it failed), `<app> · at <offset>` and right-aligned duration on a 32px header row (44px on a phone), then its error, attributes and logs as 32px rows (44px on a phone) without dividers, without repeating its row or attributes such as `code.function` that only repeat its name. Left and Right move between siblings, Up and Down to the parent or first child. The first failure starts picked. The error sits under an `error` key in the same muted key column as the attributes, its message in destructive text, and a failing log line's message is destructive. Selection stores operation, trace and span identities in the URL, never copies of live records. Back keeps the selection, moves up one level and keeps the app filter.
- GitHub Actions is the CI side: each repository is a section with GitHub's mark, each workflow an operation, a run's trace its jobs and steps; a run is named by its build number, with the attempt after a dot when it is not the first, a failed run's status names the step that failed, and a job's log lines attach to the step or job whose time holds them when `GITHUB_TOKEN` can read Actions; the step details link to GitHub's log.
- On a phone every list runs edge to edge in 44px rows with every operation column, and the trace detail pushes full-screen. The bar shows only the app's mark at the root, like every app on a phone, and Back plus the pane's title when pushed; the app marks scroll in one row beside the root's mark, the time frame stays fixed before the account, and the bar never wraps; a pushed pane hides the time frame. A phone starts with no operation selected; a desktop layout defaults to the first operation of the filtered list. Focus follows Tab, never the selected row.
- Each pane selects with one click; there is no palette, search field or page-wide key handling.
