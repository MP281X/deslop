# Portfolio

- **Profile.** Show a public profile and printable CV with no sign-in and no persisted visitor data.
- **Presence.** Show live visitor cursors, trails and two wandering bots. A tab keeps its guest identity through reloads. Tabs with the same identity share one cursor and colour until the last tab closes. When presence is unavailable, say so and keep the profile and CV in place.
- **Design.** Keep Portfolio's own fonts, hotkeys, presence text and artwork, including the 504×504 touch icon on `#fafafa`. Portfolio is exempt from the repository's app design rules.
