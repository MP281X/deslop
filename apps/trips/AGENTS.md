# Trips intent

- The list groups Now, Upcoming and Past; only a delay, cancellation or stale live answer carries a dot, and stale data carries its age. A booking detail shows departure, gate and seat once in its header.
- Flights, trains and stays keep live gates, platforms, delays and belts; the next journey also appears in the Live Activity and the widget.

## Journeys

- Copy a booking's text or screenshot, return to Trips and add it from the clipboard offer; it joins the list.
- Delete a booking by swipe or from its detail: its row waits eight seconds with Undo, then the booking leaves the server; Undo keeps it.
