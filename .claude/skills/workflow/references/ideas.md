# Idea critique

**Critique.** Challenge the outcome, taste and observed attempts independently of the author's preference. Compare different mechanisms, including a smaller one; test the strongest counterexample and weakest assumption. Prefer a reversible synthesis when it combines compatible strengths.

**Return.** Give the recommended direction and decisive reason, with the smallest observation that could change it and unresolved user constraints. Show a comparison only when alternatives are genuinely relevant; no stage plan, brainstorm inventory or mandatory report template.
