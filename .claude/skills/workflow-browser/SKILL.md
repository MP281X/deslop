# Browser and computer proof

Prove the brief's criteria at its URL with its saved state/credentials; do not read or rewrite product source or supplied prototype drivers. Reuse one session/known locators, batch independent values and wait on actual state. Capture meaningful desktop/phone states, open screenshots and judge hierarchy, clipping, density and reachable controls. Open UI directions follow design's five-version comparison; settled verification is not another prototype exercise. For interaction, record the already-open page without passing its URL again, at the default 30 fps with a contact sheet. Stop recording/profilers immediately after the last required state, before analysis; inspect meaningful frames and sequence, not duration alone. Repeat only missing proof after a tiny capture probe, not the whole journey. Read console/errors, close the session and stop owned temporary services. No exposed or retained preview unless assigned.
