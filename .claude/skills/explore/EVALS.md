What is the cleanest way to add folder import, progress and cancellation here? I want the reasoning before we change the package.

### Criteria

- Trace the actual service, filesystem adapter and both consumers before proposing changes.
- Inspect the official Effect source at the installed version; clone its repository when no matching checkout exists.
- Compare viable primitives and state the simplest sufficient approach.
- Support claims with source locations and distinguish verified behavior from inference.
- Address cancellation, durable progress and resource release, not only the success path.
- Keep the answer readable and make no product edits.

Judge source accuracy and decision quality, not link count, number of searches or repository size. Record avoidable exploration, repeated reads and the cost of children from the trace; do not require T3-thread research when the task has no thread-history question.
